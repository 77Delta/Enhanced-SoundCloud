# Privacy Policy

**The developer collects no data.** There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no servers of our own.

## What the extension does

It runs only on soundcloud.com. It reads the title, artist, link and cover of the song you are playing, so it can show lyrics, a video background and other features.

## Who your browser contacts

Your browser talks to these services directly. Nothing goes through the developer.

- **Lyrics** (if turned on): lrclib.net gets the song title and artist.
- **Video** (if turned on): Piped and Invidious servers get the song title and artist. YouTube loads the video, so it sees your IP address like on any website.
- **Lyrics or Video** (if turned on): GitHub gets a request for a public list of corrections. Nothing about you is sent.
- **Download button**: only if you click it, a new tab opens cobalt.meowing.de with the song link.

These services have their own privacy policies. Turn Lyrics and Video off and the extension contacts none of them.

## Discord status (optional)

If you install the separate helper program, the extension sends the song info to it on your own computer (`127.0.0.1`). The helper gives it to the Discord app. Discord shows it based on your Discord privacy settings. Without the helper, nothing is sent.

## Stored on your device

Your settings are saved in your browser for soundcloud.com. Uninstall the extension and clear site data to remove them.

## Permissions

Access to soundcloud.com, and a connection to your own computer for the optional Discord status. Nothing else.
